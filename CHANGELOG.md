# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first playable (October 2026)

**Core loop**
- Start homeless with $100; goal: $1 Billion net worth (then the $1 Trillion race).
- 6 physical jobs with real missions in the 3D city: package delivery, car wash, lawn mowing, timed food delivery (with tips), warehouse shifts, dock hand (unlocks after 10 jobs).
- 16 businesses across 5 tiers (taco stand → Starforge Aerospace) with 5 upgrade levels and hireable workers on salary.
- 10 real-estate listings: buy, rent out, renovate & flip, move in (+10% job pay). Per-district markets.
- Mansions with 6 luxury features, built on your own lot in the Luxury District — friends can visit online.
- 9 vehicles from a city bike to a private jet; helicopter flight; fast travel with yacht/heli/jet.
- Live stock market (AI, tech, energy, retail, real estate, space) with hourly prices, company news and charts.
- Economy cycles: boom, stable, recession, market crash — plus inflation over a 60-day game year.
- Optional crime: robberies, scams, smuggling, a bank heist — heat, fines, jail, property seizure.
- Endgame: skyscraper, rocket launch, global IPO, golden statue — visible in the city.
- 8 AI rival tycoons on the leaderboard.

**Online**
- ENet multiplayer up to 100 players: host, join by IP, or headless dedicated server.
- Server-authoritative shared economy, stock prices and clock; chat; money transfers; corporations (teams) with a corporate leaderboard; server-side net-worth anti-cheat.

**Live-ops (cosmetic only, not pay-to-win)**
- Empire Coins, daily reward, 30-tier Season Pass with free + premium tracks, character skins, car paints, mansion decor.

**Tech**
- Godot 4.3, GL Compatibility renderer (desktop, web, Android).
- Procedural city (MultiMesh batching), day/night cycle, traffic and pedestrians.
- Synthesized music & SFX (no audio files).
- Keyboard/mouse, gamepad and touch controls.
- Signed save files, autosave.
- 418 unit tests + a balance bot that must reach $1B; CI builds Android/Windows/Linux/Web/server and publishes a release + website.
