A living economy

Booms. Crashes. Opportunity.

The economy cycles on its own — online, everyone lives through the same cycle at the same time. Inflation pushes prices up over every 60-day game year.

BOOM

Business income +25%, assets +10%, stocks rally. Sell properties into strength.

STABLE

Steady growth, normal prices. Build and upgrade.

RECESSION

Income −20%, property values slide. Keep cash ready.

MARKET CRASH

Stocks plunge, assets −28%. Fortunes are made by whoever buys now.

FAQ

Questions

Is it pay-to-win?

No. The only things you can unlock with Empire Coins are cosmetics — skins, car paints, mansion decor and the Season Pass. Nothing changes your money, speed or rank. Real-money purchases aren't enabled in this build.

How long does it take to become a billionaire?

A focused player gets there in roughly 90–110 in-game days (an in-game day is 3 real minutes, and single-player can run at ×2 or ×4). Timing the market cycles gets you there faster.

How do I play with friends?

On PC or Android: Phone → Online → Host, and friends join with your IP. For internet play, forward UDP port 7777 or run the dedicated server: ./MoneyEmpireServer.x86_64 --server. The browser version is single-player (browsers block the UDP networking the game uses).

Controls?

WASD to move, Shift to run, E to interact, Tab for the phone, V to hop in your vehicle, T to chat, drag the mouse to look. Full gamepad support. On phones: joystick on the left, drag on the right to look.

Can I mod or extend it?

Yes — it's an MIT-licensed Godot 4.3 project. Every price and income lives in scripts/core/catalog.gd. See docs/GODOT.md in the repo.
